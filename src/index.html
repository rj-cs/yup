Why an indie software lab?

Modern software often feels heavy—wrapped in mandatory cloud accounts, sluggish Electron wrappers, and recurring subscriptions for tools that could be a 4MB static binary or a single local-first browser tab.

I built yupcode.com as a permanent home for my weekend code experiments, developer utilities, and systems deep-dives.

  • 01
    Local-First by Default: Your data stays in SQLite, IndexedDB, or your local filesystem.
  • 02
    Sub-Millisecond Ergonomics: Keyboard-first UIs, zero layout shift, and measurable latency budgets.
  • 03
    Radical Simplicity: Ship small, sharp tools that do one job exceptionally well.
